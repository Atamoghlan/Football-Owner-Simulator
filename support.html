Frequently Asked Questions

Is my career saved automatically?
Yes. The game saves automatically as you play and when you leave it.

Will updating the game delete my career?
No. Updates keep your career. Uninstalling the game deletes the career, because it is stored only on your device.

Do I need the internet to play?
No. You can play without the internet. It is only needed for videos and purchases.

I reinstalled the game or got a new phone. What happens to what I bought?
Credits, builder crews, world-class players and coaches and the unspent part of the club money you bought come back on their own within a couple of minutes, if iCloud is on and you use the same Apple ID (on Android, if device backup is on). You can also open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom to get permanent purchases and the active subscription back from the store.

I started a new career. Do I lose my purchases?
No. Credits, crews and bought players and coaches go with you, and so does the unspent part of the club money you bought.

I don't see a video button.
The button appears only when a video is ready. Sometimes the ad network has no video to offer, or there is no internet. The button comes back by itself as soon as a video arrives. You can always pay with credits instead where the game offers this.

How do I cancel the Owner's Box?
In your App Store or Google Play account settings, under subscriptions. See the Terms of Use.

What languages does the game support?
English and Russian. The game starts in your device language, and you can change it in Settings.

I found a bug or have a suggestion — what should I do?
Email us at dreamgamesllc@gmail.com with as much detail as possible: what happened, what you expected, and your device model. We read every message.
