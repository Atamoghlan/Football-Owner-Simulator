Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Football Owner Simulator ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our game on iOS and Android.

Summary: We do not collect any personal data ourselves. The game needs no account and keeps your career on your device. Optional reward videos are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect some data as described below. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google. What you bought is also kept in your own iCloud or Google backup so that it is not lost.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information. The App does not:

  • Require account creation, sign-in or any personal details (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Send your career or settings to us or to any server of ours
  • Access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows only optional reward videos that you choose to watch. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To load and show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier (on iOS only if you allow tracking)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Information about ad views and interactions
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information

On iOS, the App asks for permission through App Tracking Transparency; you can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in your device's Settings. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states), the App shows Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later in the game: Settings → Ad privacy.

Google's use of data is described here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Purchases

Credits, club money, world-class players and coaches, builder crews, the weekly pass and the Owner's Box subscription are sold as in-app purchases. Payments are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never receive your payment details. You can restore permanent purchases in the game: Shop → Restore Purchases.

4. Data Stored on Your Device and in Your Own Backup

The App saves your career, credits, purchases and settings such as language, music and sound locally on your device.

  • On iOS, a copy of your balance and of what you bought (no personal details) is kept in your own iCloud account through Apple's key-value storage, so that purchases come back after you reinstall the game or move to a new iPhone with the same Apple ID.
  • On Android, the game's files may be included in your device's automatic backup to your Google account, if you have this turned on.
  • This data is not accessible to us. You control iCloud and Google backups in your device's settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
